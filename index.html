Save, share, record, MIDI

Recording captures the master bus in your browser (WebM). MIDI export writes every note played since you pressed Play. Share links carry the whole system — same link, same music. MIDI OUT sends notes on channels 1–8 (tracks 1–8) plus 24 PPQ clock and start/stop: on a Mac, enable the IAC Driver (Audio MIDI Setup), pick it here, and in Ableton set that input's Track (and Sync, if you want Ableton to follow this tempo) on.

Guide

Start: press the big PLAY button at the top. It is already making music — a seed completely determines the system, so New seed is a new piece with the same settings.

Tracks: each track is a generator (where notes come from) × an engine (what they sound like), running at its own step length — that's the polymeter. Click steps in a track's grid to force a step ON (blue ring), force it OFF (red ring), or back to AUTO (third click).

Sound: open Sound design on any track for a full ADSR, filter, drive, glide and two engine knobs — their names change with the engine (FM gets Ratio / Mod amount, Pluck gets Damping / Brightness, and so on). Audition plays the track once, right now.

Change: the Evolution Lab decides what the machine may rewrite — rhythm, pitch, density, length, engine, sound knobs, harmony, FX — how wild, how often, and in which direction. Conditional mutation is per-track: when a track fires, it can reach over and mutate the next track or a random one. Everything it does is written in the Evolution Log, and Undo takes the last change back.

Form: the Form Director divides your target length into sections and morphs between them. Build your own arc in the Custom arc editor — including sections that recall an earlier section's exact state, so themes return.

Honest limits: one voice per track, like the PyGamer (new notes cut the old one on that track). CHORD plays a real triad in the browser; over MIDI it sends the root note, like the PyGamer. Pluck/String are true Karplus-Strong, computed per note. Web MIDI needs Chrome/Edge and your permission.
